The Story

DJ Side.B has been lighting up dance floors with chatpata Bollywood remixes and desi indie beats — from baraat to after-party, to club night or a private party. Every set is curated to the room, the moment, and the crowd in front of it.

Behind DJ Side.B is Dhiresh — an engineer by profession, a two-time founder, and currently a full-time Entrepreneur-in-Residence at a startup in Gurugram. In his pursuit of finding happiness in music, he took up DJing, and what began as a personal escape from spreadsheets and start-up hours slowly turned into sets, gigs, and a name of its own.
